BLUEPRINT // TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE

A JOURNEY WORTH PLAYING.
FOUR ROUNDS. ONE CHAMPION.

RITHMOS 2026 brings together the fiercest live acts across rock, metal, indie, blues, and fusion. Here is your roadmap from demo intake to headline arena status.

01 // THE ROADMAP

THE 4 TOURNAMENT STAGES

Every band earns their way through real live performances in front of unsparing juries and passionate crowds.

ROUND 01

DEMO INTAKE

Submit your original jam or rehearsal audio. Curated by experienced Hyderabad rock veterans. 48 bands shortlisted.

ROUND 02

CLUB QUALIFIERS

15-minute high-octane sets across top live music venues in Hyderabad. Sound engineers evaluate raw stage presence. Top 24 advance.

ROUND 03

CITY SEMIFINALS

Head-to-head knockouts. 20-minute showcase featuring live jury scoring and real-time audience voting. 6 finalists survive.

ROUND 04

THE GRAND FINALE

Headline performance on the massive RITHMOS stadium arena stage before 10,000 screaming fans. Live 4K broadcast.

02 // JURY EVALUATION

TRANSPARENT SCORING WEIGHTS

Our judging is based on musical integrity, tightness, and stage charisma.

30%

ORIGINALITY & COMPOSITION

Songwriting craftsmanship, harmonic structure, lyricism, and distinct musical identity.

30%

TECHNICAL MUSICIANSHIP

Rhythm section cohesion, timing tightness, vocal execution, dynamic control, and balance.

25%

STAGE ENERGY & COMMAND

Confidence under the lights, audience engagement, movement, and commanding stage personality.

15%

CROWD DECIBEL VOTE

Real-time audience roar and live voting from fans in the pit.

04 // ELIGIBILITY & RULES

WHO CAN COMPETE

BAND REQUIREMENTS

• Band must consist of at least 2 members with live instruments (drums, guitars, bass, keys, brass, strings).
• Pre-recorded backing tracks, backing vocals, or click-stem automation are strictly prohibited.
• All genres of live music welcome: Rock, Metal, Indie, Blues, Fusion, Progressive, Alternative.

SONGWRITING & FEES

• 100% Free Entry: No registration fees, audition charges, or backstage passes costs.
• Bands must perform original compositions (one cover permitted in Round 02 club qualifier).
• All original rights remain 100% with the songwriters.
