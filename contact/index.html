Bands must bring their own guitars, basses, effect pedalboards, drum cymbals, snare drum, and drum sticks.

While one cover song is allowed during the 20-minute qualifier round, 70% of judging points are directly tied to original songwriting, arrangement, and lyrical creativity. RITHMOS was created to discover the next generation of original rock songs.

Yes. Bands from any city in India are eligible to apply, provided they can attend their scheduled live qualifier round in Hyderabad. Travel and accommodation stipends are provided to all outstation bands that advance to the Top 8 Arena Finale.

Judging is 100% transparent and standardized across 4 categories: Musicianship & Tightness (25%), Originality & Composition (25%), Stage Energy & Performance (25%), and FOH Sound Balance & Vocal Clarity (25%). Detailed rubric scorecards are provided to each band after their set.
